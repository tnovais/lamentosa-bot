Hunt Enemies - PvP

Here you can attack other players and if you win, you'll steal 5 to 10% of their gold.

Attacking other players is a fundamental part of Lamentosa, as it's one of the best ways to collect gold and you need gold to upgrade your attributes.

Likewise, other players can attack you and steal the same 5 to 10%, so be prepared, because Lamentosa is cruel.

There is a form of immunity, with each PvP attack you can't be attacked for 60 minutes, so if you're collecting gold it's a good idea to carry out at least 1 attack every 50 minutes.

With every PvP victory you also get 1 grimoire point. Grimoire points are used to evolve spells at level 10, so accumulate as many as you can.
You can see the number of grimoire points under "My character", it's a book icon below your picture.
